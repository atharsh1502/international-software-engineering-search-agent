Visa sponsorship & application safety

Singapore

Employment Pass applications are employer-filed and must meet salary/COMPASS eligibility. Some employers explicitly prioritize applicants who already have Singapore work rights. Ask the recruiter before spending time on assessments.

MOM EP eligibility rules

Malaysia

Foreign applicants cannot self-apply for an Employment Pass; the employing company must submit the expatriate pass application. Confirm the employer is willing and eligible to sponsor.

Malaysia MYXpats/ESD EP FAQ

UAE / GCC

For UAE private-sector work, employer applies for the work permit/standard work visa. Sponsorship language is role-specific; confirm costs, relocation, client identity and written offer terms.

UAE official work permit information

Recruitment safety

Never pay a recruiter, consultant or employer for a job offer, visa processing, security deposit, training fee or interview slot. Verify agency/client and contract before sharing passport or bank details.
